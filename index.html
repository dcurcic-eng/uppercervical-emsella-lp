11You probably have questions

We answered the ones our competitors avoid.

Does Emsella actually improve intimacy?+

Yes. Patients consistently report stronger orgasms, easier and deeper arousal, more sensation, and better vaginal tone after a full series. The same muscle group governs sexual response and continence; strengthening one tends to strengthen the other. Many couples notice the change first.

Does it actually hurt?+

No. You feel a deep tingling and the pelvic muscles contracting on their own. Some patients say the sensation is unfamiliar the first 30 seconds, but never painful. We dial intensity to your comfort level and can lower it at any point.

Is it safe? Side effects?+

Emsella is FDA cleared and noninvasive. Most patients experience no side effects. A few report mild muscle fatigue after the first session, similar to a hard workout. There is no anesthesia, no incision, and no medication.

Is Emsella covered by insurance?+

Not yet in most plans. We accept FSA and HSA dollars, and we offer CareCredit at 0% APR for 6 or 12 months. The full series total is below most surgical co pays.

When will I notice a change?+

Most patients feel something real between session 3 and 4. Intimacy changes are often the first to surface; bladder control follows. Some patients notice a subtle shift after session one. Peer reviewed data shows 68% symptom improvement at six months.

How many sessions will I need?+

The clinical protocol is six 28 minute sessions over three weeks. More established cases may add two to six maintenance sessions. We tell you which after your first visit.

Does it help with leakage too?+

Yes. Emsella is FDA cleared for stress, urge, and mixed urinary incontinence in women and men. Leaks during laughing, lifting, sneezing, and running typically reduce dramatically across the full series, often alongside the intimate improvements.

Can men use Emsella?+

Yes, fully clothed and on the same chair. Men post prostatectomy and men with stress incontinence respond well; many also report improvements to erectile function and stamina.
